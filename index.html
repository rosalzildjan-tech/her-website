Hobbies

I mean, I like to do a lot of things. One of the hobbies that I like to do is music. Seems vague enough, but I like doing things that revolve around music, from composing my own music to collecting vinyl records. Ever since I was in elementary school, I have always liked creating songs, even before I learned how to play any instrument. I also have to admit that I have a “love-hate” relationship with music. From an early age, it caused me trauma, but I eventually grew to like it (let me tell you in detail next time; this may be too deep of a conversation to write down haha).

Anyways, I think I have written almost a hundred songs and tried to produce some of them, but I have always had a habit of not finishing my compositions. It may be because I would like them to remain as ideas and potential (deep hahah). I also started collecting vinyl records in 2024. I don’t have a lot in my collection, but enough to call myself a collector. I plan to expand it, but this collecting hobby is expensive haha. Another hobby related to music is driving around with an awesome playlist. Seems really cool.

Educational background

I think my educational background is mediocre. I worked hard and didn’t flunk any classes, but I would say I wasn’t the best at it. Some information about me: my strand was STEM, and my college course was BS in Psychology. Why Psychology? I don’t know either, but I guess when I was in senior high school, I struggled with my mental health a bit, and I guess psychology was one of the ways I could figure things out and help myself. Later on, I grew to like it because it’s a helping profession.

Currently, I’m working on my master’s degree to become a psychologist. Hopefully, I can find enough motivation to finish my thesis.

Aspirations

I have always lived by one motto: “Exist.” It’s too hard to explain, but what I aspire to be is significant. This may seem narcissistic, but I hope not. I don’t aspire to have prestige; I want my existence to matter. I don’t want to walk this earth living just like any other guy. I want to live a significant life by doing things that matter.

That’s why I strive to finish my education and become a psychologist. I think that mental health is a noble pursuit and that it impacts people deeply. I also aspire to introduce systemic change that benefits people, especially those who experience abuse.

A year ago, I started Project Alamat, an advocacy project that helps address the literacy problem in our country. I want to help children get a good education and equip themselves to become great professionals in the future.

I know I’m idealistic, and it has its benefits as well as its downsides. But I have always wanted to live a life that is about more than just myself, and I don’t think I’ll change this mentality.
